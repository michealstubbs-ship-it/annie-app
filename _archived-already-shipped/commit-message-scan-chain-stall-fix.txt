Fix a real, reproducible bug found by a 19-scenario staged first-scan audit: concurrent first scans can silently stall forever at round 1 with zero signals

Ran 19 synthetic first-scan signups on staging (annie-staging Supabase
project, test-payments branch deploy) covering 13 location/sector/function
profiles across UAE-GCC/UK/US -- including deliberately niche and harder
cases (Government/NGO, Automotive+Education, Real Estate, Luxury/Fashion,
Law/Regulatory) -- with 3 flagship profiles also run at Starter/Growth/Team
to compare tiers. No production customers or billing were touched; all
accounts and their data have been cleaned up from staging afterward.

THE BUG (found, root-caused, fixed):
4 of the 19 scans -- all Growth-tier, all UAE/GCC, all fired within about
15 seconds of each other -- came back with EXACTLY ZERO signals after
running out their full tier ceiling, with nothing in error_logs anywhere.
Root cause, confirmed directly in scan-now-background.js: fireNextRound()
(the fire-and-forget call a scan makes to chain itself into its next
research round) only ever guarded against the fetch() PROMISE itself
rejecting (a DNS/network failure) -- it never checked the response it got
back. Netlify's own gateway rejecting a request under a concurrent-
invocation burst (a 429/503) resolves fetch() normally rather than
throwing, so that failure was completely invisible: the chain just
silently stopped after round 1, and scan-status.js's own staleness check
was the only thing that ever surfaced it, many minutes later, with no
root cause attached anywhere.

This isn't just a test artifact -- it's the same shape of event a real
marketing push, a webinar, or a few signups from the same company within
minutes of each other would produce, and it means some fraction of those
new customers could land on a genuinely empty first-scan dashboard with
zero visibility into why, until they contact support or churn.

THE FIX: netlify/functions/scan-now-background.js's fireNextRound() now
checks the response status. A non-OK response gets one immediate retry
(still inside the same unawaited promise chain this function already
relies on completing post-return -- no new timer introduced, nothing this
runtime hasn't already proven it lets finish). A repeat failure after the
retry now calls reportServerError, same as every other failure path in
this file, instead of vanishing.

New tests (netlify/functions/tests/scan-now-background.test.js):
fireNextRound is now exported specifically for direct unit testing against
a mocked global.fetch (reaching it through the full handler would mean
mocking the entire research pipeline, which is scanShared.js's own tested
territory) -- covers success, retry-then-success, retry-then-still-failing
(reports), and a genuine network-level throw (reports).

OTHER AUDIT FINDINGS (not code bugs, logged for awareness / future
follow-up, not touched in this pass):
- Across the 15 scenarios unaffected by the bug, actual Feed signal counts
  ranged 6-14 -- every profile got SOMETHING, including the niche ones
  (Government/NGO, Automotive, Luxury/Fashion all produced real signals).
  Niche/unusual sector-function combinations were not, by themselves, a
  predictor of a thin scan.
- However, only 6 of those 15 scenarios actually hit their tier's
  Today's-BD-Actions target (a Feed full of funding/expansion/M&A signals
  doesn't automatically mean a full Actions list, since only
  leadership_change/live_job count there) -- most notably the UK
  Automotive+Education/Training niche profile got 7 Feed signals but ZERO
  actionable ones, and the US Tech/AI&Data profile got 11 Feed signals
  (6 of them funding) but found no live_job entries at all. Worth a closer
  look in a future pass, not fixed here.
- Contact-verification hit rate was solid where the signal type was
  eligible: ~89% for leadership_change, ~79% for live_job, across the
  full 19-scenario dataset.

Verification: 812 tests passing (up from 808), npm run build clean.

UX/UI audit (27 Aug), applying the taste-skill review methodology to the live app plus a targeted bug hunt. Two isolated fixes, both verified against production directly before being written:

1. Billing page was advertising two fabricated paid perks. The Growth plan card listed "LinkedIn re-import on demand" and "Priority support" as Growth-exclusive; the Team card listed "Shared target-company list." None of these are real: LinkedIn re-import has no tier check anywhere in the code and works for anyone regardless of plan; there's no priority support queue anywhere in the codebase, every escalation from every tier goes to the one inbox support-escalate.js writes to; and there's no target-company-list concept left in the product at all. Confirmed directly against the live site: fetched the actual deployed pricing-*.js chunk from app.meetannie.ai and it contained these exact fabricated strings, so this wasn't a stale-cache false alarm, it's what paying customers see today. It also directly contradicts what Annie's own support chat already tells a customer who asks (SupportWidget.jsx's system prompt is explicit that neither is a real paid perk) — a customer could get two different, contradictory answers about the same plan depending on whether they read the pricing page or asked support. Fixed by dropping both fabricated Growth bullets (replaced the vaguer "Deeper onboarding research pass" wording with "Deeper, ongoing research scans," matching what the scan-tier config actually does on an ongoing basis, not just at signup) and replacing Team's fabricated bullet with the real, genuinely Team-exclusive perk it was presumably gesturing at: a shared CRM across the whole team.

2. Ask Annie's chat page can render its Send button off-screen on any phone or narrow tablet (under the 768px breakpoint). Chat.jsx's outer container is a fixed `h-screen` (100vh), but on that breakpoint Dashboard.jsx's <main> already reserves 56px at the top for a fixed mobile header bar (its own `pt-14 md:pt-0`). Chat.jsx's container never accounted for that, so on mobile it renders 56px taller than the space actually visible below the header, pushing the message input row (with the Send button) 56px below the fold, reachable only by scrolling <main> itself first. Fixed by matching Dashboard's own pattern: `h-[calc(100vh-3.5rem)]` below the md breakpoint, `md:h-screen` at and above it, same 3.5rem (56px) Dashboard.jsx already uses for its top padding.

Both fixes are pure copy/CSS-class changes, no logic touched. 892/892 tests passing, production build clean.

Two fixes from tonight's pre-launch QA exercise, both found by actually running the product as 5 different real test customers rather than just reading code.

1. Ask Annie's chat answers were not web-search grounded, and could read as confidently current when they were actually stale. chat.js has supported a `webSearch` flag end-to-end since the scan pipeline's own web-search work (same Anthropic web_search tool, no new integration) -- but Chat.jsx never turned it on. Live-tested on 27 Aug 2026: asked "what's happening in the UK legal market right now", Annie answered from what reads as roughly 18-month-stale training data (referencing "early 2025... new administration"), while the real scan pipeline, asked the same day, was correctly querying for e.g. "Technology funding rounds United States August 2026". New src/lib/chatWebSearch.js (tested) adds a small keyword heuristic -- shouldSearchWeb(message) -- so search only turns on for questions that actually need current information ("what's happening", "latest", "recently", "this week", "has X announced/launched/raised/acquired/hired"), not every message: search adds real per-message cost and latency, and most Ask Annie questions (draft this email, prep me for this call) don't need it. Chat.jsx now passes webSearch/maxSearchUses through on that basis, and renders a "Checked live just now" source list under any answer that actually triggered a search, so a recruiter can tell a grounded answer from Annie's own knowledge rather than taking "as of today" on faith.

2. Government & Public Sector's TARGET_FIRM_DIRECTORY entry (scanShared.js) -- confirmed genuinely thin during the QA exercise: a real scan for this sector timed out short of its target with zero funding/leadership/live-job signals found, and annie_learned_sources showed ZERO of Annie's own AI-discovered companies for it, only what a test account's own CRM taught her. First draft of this fix anchored on public-affairs/lobbying consultancies and federal IT contractors (APCO, FTI, Booz Allen, Leidos, etc.) -- caught in review that those are consulting firms with government CLIENTS, not the government/public-sector employers this sector is actually meant to represent. Corrected anchors are genuine government, semi-government and multilateral bodies across the three markets in scope: UK (Cabinet Office, HM Treasury, Home Office, Ofcom, Competition and Markets Authority), UAE (Federal Authority for Government Human Resources, Dubai Future Foundation, Government Development and The Future Office, Mohammed Bin Rashid School of Government -- all verified real), and US/multilateral (General Services Administration, World Bank, IMF). This sector also doesn't map onto "funding/expansion" signals the way consulting or law firms do -- a government department doesn't raise a funding round -- so the discoveryHint points at leadership-appointment coverage instead, and deliberately reuses the real, already-wired-in regional press this sector already had in REGIONAL_SOURCE_DIRECTORY (Civil Service World for the UK, WAM for UAE/GCC, GovExec/Route Fifty for the US) rather than inventing a second, parallel source list that could drift out of sync.

Both changes: 888/888 tests pass (4 new, for shouldSearchWeb), production build clean.
